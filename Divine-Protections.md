# Divine Protections (加護)

> Blessings granted by Od Laguna at birth (or inherited in rare cases).  
> Far weaker than Authorities. No Divine Protection can overpower a true Authority. A Witch Factor can remove preexisting Divine Protections.  
> Resolution: usually automatic when conditions are met; contested only when the effect allows resistance.

## Key Divine Protections

### Divine Protection of the Sword Saint
**Holder:** Successive members of House Astrea (currently Reinhard).  
**Effect:** Inherited. Previous holder instinctively knows the successor. Dramatically elevates swordsmanship and grants the right to wield the Dragon Sword Reid.  
**Notes:** Reid Astrea himself did not possess this Protection.

### Divine Protection of Wind Reading
**Holder:** Crusch Karsten.  
**Effect:** Read the wind, see invisible things, and sense emotions / detect lies.

### Divine Protection of Wind Evasion
**Holders:** Earth dragons (and Reinhard).  
**Effect:** Ignore wind resistance; applies to any vehicle the dragon pulls. Cooldown after use.

### Divine Protection of the Soul Language (言霊の加護)
**Holder:** Otto Suwen.  
**Effect:** Communicate with animals and certain non-human beings via soul language.

### Divine Protection of the Death God (residual)
**Historical:** Theresia van Astrea.  
**Effect:** Wounds inflicted cannot be healed by normal means.

### Reinhard’s Multi-Protection Suite
Reinhard possesses an absurd number of Divine Protections (dozens to hundreds). Notable ones include:
- Sword Saint (above)
- Wind Evasion
- Various resistances (poison, disease, fire, arrows, etc.)
- Training mastery, riding mastery, unarmed mastery
- Baking / cooking related (humorous but functional)
- Phoenix (revival-related)
- Judgment, Telepathy, and many more.

Exact full list is **UNCERTAIN** and treated as “effectively whatever is needed within reason for a Peak character.”

### Other Notable Protections
- **Detoxification / Panacea / Child of Wind**: Disease and poison resistance/immunity.
- **Shedding Blood**: Grows stronger as the user bleeds.
- **Drafts**: Knows escape routes.
- **Telepathy**, **Empathy**, **Evaluation**, **First Sight**, **Speed**, **Spirit Gathering**, **Magic Resistance**, **Earth Resistance**, etc.

## Rules for Play
- Automatic when the stated condition is met.
- Can be suppressed or removed by Witch Factors / Authorities.
- Multiple Protections on one person are rare and marked as exceptional (only Reinhard and Vague Adgard are known to hold more than one in significant numbers).
