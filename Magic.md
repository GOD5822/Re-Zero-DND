# Magic

> Cast / control: `5d4 + floor(Mana Mastery / 3)`.  
> Mana Talent governs raw potential and learning speed. Higher Talent always wins magical potential; Mastery governs control and efficiency.  
> Incantations are traditional but not strictly required for experienced users.

## Elements
- **Fire (火)**: Temperature — flames or ice (by lowering temperature). Incantation base: **Goa**.
- **Water (水)**: Life force interference & healing. Incantation base: **Shiha** / **Huma** (shared with Fire for ice).
- **Wind (風)**: Atmosphere manipulation, external effects. Incantation base: **Fura**.
- **Earth (土)**: Internal body / land effects, fertilization or starvation of land. Incantation base: **Dona**.
- **Yin (陰)**: Abstract — time/space, senses, gravity, separation. Rare. Incantation base: **Shamak**.
- **Yang (陽)**: Abstract — enhancement, light, piercing/severing. Rarest and least developed. Incantation base: **Jiwald**.

### Ice Special Note
- Fire method: freezes existing moisture → less mana, less control, ice remains.
- Water method: creates new water-as-ice → more mana, full control, can disperse.

## Strength Prefixes
Base spell → **El** → **Ul** → **Al** (increasing power, scale, and complexity).

Examples:
- Goa → El Goa → Ul Goa → Al Goa
- Shamak (sense separation) → stronger versions that separate more of the target from the world → **Al Shamak** (rift to alternate plane)

## Healing Magic
Primarily Water affinity. Top-tier healers (Felix, etc.) can restore limbs, cure complex conditions, and stabilize near-death states. Healing is contested or automatic depending on severity and the caster’s Mastery.

## Other Rules
- Mental image + construction required. Complex spells take longer to form.
- Emotional turmoil can cause loss of control (e.g. Emilia’s Permafrost of Elior Forest).
- Yin and Yang conflict: advancing one hinders the other.
- Yang enhancement often leaves the target feeling like they are controlling someone else’s body (major drawback).
- Magic can be combined with martial arts by skilled users (casting from limbs, eyes, tongue, etc.).

## Mandatory Fields for Spell Entries
Name & element • User/affinity • Governing stat (usually Mana Mastery) & roll • Effect & automatic clause • Cost (mana) / limitations / counterplay • Lore & scaling.
