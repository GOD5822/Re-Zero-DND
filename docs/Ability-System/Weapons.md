# Weapons

> Wielder attacks use **Strength** unless the weapon states Agility.  
> Multiply the listed base modifier by `50% + 1.5% × Weapon Proficiency` (max 200% at WP 100).  
> Contested attacks use `5d4 + floor(governing stat/3)`.  
> Absolute effects (e.g. “cuts the uncuttable”) bypass the roll when conditions are met.

## Legendary Enchanted Swords (Maken)

### Dragon Sword Reid
**Name:** Dragon Sword Reid (龍剣レイド)  
**Wielder(s):** Reinhard van Astrea (current); historically Reid Astrea and successive Sword Saints of House Astrea.  
**Description:** The strongest and sharpest sword in existence. Forged with reason foreign to the world and blessed by the Sword God. Indestructible; its sheath (marked by Volcanica’s claws) is equally indestructible. Can only be drawn against a “worthy” opponent (criteria unknown and independent of raw power). Once drawn, it has never been sheathed without cutting something. Said to cut that which cannot be cut and, in extreme cases, rewrite local reality.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength attack; base modifier **+5,000** (proficiency-scaled). When drawn, the attack is treated as absolute against physical and most magical defenses. Worthiness is automatic and controlled by the sword itself. Cannot be drawn against non-worthy targets.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Can only be drawn under the sword’s own judgment. Extreme narrative weight when drawn.  
**Lore & Scaling:** Peak relic that anchors Reinhard’s Tier 0 status.

### Yang Sword Vollachia
**Name:** Yang Sword Vollachia (陽剣ヴォラキア)  
**Wielder(s):** Qualified members of the Vollachian imperial family (Priscilla Barielle, Vincent Vollachia, Eugard Vollachia, etc.). Unworthy wielders are burned to death (or lose the offending limb).  
**Description:** The imperial flame sword. Uses the sky itself as its sheath and can be manifested from thin air. Can cut and burn whatever the wielder wishes, including the soul. Multiple instances can exist simultaneously (though each is weakened). Boosts the wielder’s physical capabilities dramatically.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength attack; base modifier **+1,200** (proficiency-scaled). On a successful hit (or graze), the wielder may choose to burn the target’s body, soul, or both. Soul-burn is an automatic effect once contact is made. Physical capability boost is automatic while the sword is drawn.  
**Cost / Limitations:** High continuous mana drain while drawn. Multiple concurrent instances dilute power. Rejects unworthy users automatically (lethal).  
**Lore & Scaling:** Apex political and combat relic of Vollachia; below Dragon Sword Reid.

### Fiend Sword Murasame
**Name:** Fiend Sword Murasame (邪剣ムラサメ)  
**Wielder(s):** Cecilus Segmunt (current, reforged into katana form by Groovy Gumlet).  
**Description:** An Enchanted Sword of extreme cruelty and bloodlust. Severs the perceived “point” (essence) of anything—people, objects, phenomena, concepts, curses, or contracts. Can fling long-range slashes. Calls for blood and death; requires suitable compensation (often life-threatening for average users).  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength or Agility attack; base modifier **+2,000** (proficiency-scaled). On hit, the wielder may sever a non-physical quality (curse, contract, scent, conceptual link, etc.) as an absolute effect. Long-range slash option available.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Demands frequent use as a killing tool. High energy drain for non-compatible users. Hates Groovy Gumlet and refuses his touch.  
**Lore & Scaling:** Apex-compatible through Cecilus; not a Peak relic.

### Dream Sword Masayume
**Name:** Dream Sword Masayume (夢剣マサユメ)  
**Wielder(s):** Cecilus Segmunt.  
**Description:** Grants (or devours) the wielder’s dreams and motivation. Uses dreams (including waking flashbacks) as its sheath. Can sever in a manner similar but not identical to Murasame. Emits extreme bloodlust even while sheathed.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Agility attack; base modifier **+2,000** (proficiency-scaled). Dream-grant/devour is automatic on extended ownership unless the wielder’s yearning is extraordinary (Cecilus qualifies). Can cut through conceptual instability.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Devours the dreams/motivation of ordinary wielders. Requires blood and sacrifice like other Enchanted Swords.  
**Lore & Scaling:** Apex-compatible through Cecilus.

### Life Sword Zeam
**Name:** Life Sword Zeam (命剣ゼアム)  
**Wielder(s):** Currently unclaimed in main canon (appears in non-canon Lost in Memories).  
**Description:** Fed with vast mana; capable of killing a person’s very soul. A soul severed by Zeam cannot be reached even by the Yang Sword.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength attack; base modifier **+1,500** (proficiency-scaled). Soul-kill is automatic on a confirmed hit against a living target.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Extreme rarity; exact current location unknown.  
**Lore & Scaling:** Apex soul-killing relic.

### Other Enchanted Swords (incomplete data)
- **Curse Swords Aeon**: Twin light blades that drain mana and absorb opponent power (primarily shown vs spirits). High consumption, short battles only.  
- **Beloved Sword, Spirit Sword, Shadow Sword, Plague Sword**: Names known; abilities, locations, and users unrevealed in main canon. Marked **UNCERTAIN**.

## Signature Arms

### Rem’s Morningstar
**Name:** Rem’s Morningstar (chain spiked mace)  
**Wielder(s):** Rem  
**Description:** Long-chain spiked morningstar used with Oni strength and Water magic support.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength; base **+120**. Trip/pull is an opposed Strength contest. Can be combined with water magic for area control.  
**Lore & Scaling:** High in Rem’s (horned) hands.

### Guiltywhip
**Name:** Guiltywhip  
**Wielder(s):** Natsuki Subaru  
**Description:** Tactical whip for movement, rescue, disarming, and creating openings.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Agility; base **+15**. Can disarm, trip, or assist instead of dealing damage.  
**Lore & Scaling:** Mid utility weapon.

### Elsa’s Kukri Knives
**Name:** Elsa’s Kukri Knives  
**Wielder(s):** Elsa Granhiert  
**Description:** Paired curved knives optimized for bleeding and rapid successive cuts.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Agility; base **+180** each. Bleeding is a Fortitude contest after a successful hit.  
**Lore & Scaling:** High due to Elsa’s skill and regeneration-exploiting style.

### Garfiel’s Shield-Gauntlets
**Name:** Garfiel’s Shield-Gauntlets  
**Wielder(s):** Garfiel Tinsel  
**Description:** Defensive striking gauntlets that double as shields.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength; base **+220**. Scaled modifier may aid one Fortitude block per round.  
**Lore & Scaling:** High defensive weapon.

### Wilhelm’s Broadswords
**Name:** Wilhelm’s Broadswords  
**Wielder(s):** Wilhelm van Astrea (current)  
**Description:** Ordinary high-quality blades elevated purely by master swordsmanship.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength or Agility; base **+250**.  
**Lore & Scaling:** High because of Wilhelm, not intrinsic divinity.

### Crusch’s Broadsword
**Name:** Crusch’s Broadsword  
**Wielder(s):** Crusch Karsten  
**Description:** Sword used with the wind-cutting technique (Divine Protection of Wind Reading support).  
**Mechanical Effect:** Strength; base **+140**. Wind technique uses Mana Mastery for extended reach/cutting.  
**Lore & Scaling:** High commander weapon.

### Julius’s Knight Rapier
**Name:** Julius’s Knight Rapier  
**Wielder(s):** Julius Juukulius  
**Description:** Rapier used in perfect coordination with six quasi-spirits.  
**Mechanical Effect:** Agility; base **+160**. Contracted spirit adds a Mastery-governed elemental rider.  
**Lore & Scaling:** High through coordination.

### Additional Signature Arms
- **Theresia’s sword (historical)**: Elevated by Divine Protection of the Sword Saint and residual Death God effects.  
- **Clind’s weapons**: High Magical Strength support; details **UNCERTAIN** beyond Base Stats.  
- **Halibel’s claws/weapons**: Martial artist style; temporary use of Murasame noted in canon.
