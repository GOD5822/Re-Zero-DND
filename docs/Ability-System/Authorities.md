# Authorities

> Witch Factors create personal rule-breaking powers.  
> **Automatic effects** bypass `5d4` when their stated conditions are met.  
> All other uses name a governing stat and roll `5d4 + floor(stat/3)`.  
> No Divine Protection can overpower a true Authority. Authorities are superior versions of Divine Protections and can remove preexisting ones.

## Core Rules
- Source is always a Witch Factor (or rare exception).
- Compatibility and the user’s nature/desires shape the exact manifestation.
- Authorities can share portions of the Factor (Apostles / “fingers”), but this usually weakens the original user’s power until the portion returns.
- Temptation: the Factor constantly pushes the user toward excess; strong self-identity is required to resist.

## Return by Death (Envy-linked) — Natsuki Subaru
**Source:** Authority linked to the Witch of Envy / Satella. Exact mechanism **UNCERTAIN**.  
**Resolution:** Automatic reset to a previous “save point” on death. Subaru does not consciously choose the checkpoint.  
**Effect:** Full temporal reset of the world state to the last save. Only Subaru retains memories (with trauma).  
**Cost / Limitations:** Extreme mental trauma, isolation, and a taboo that prevents speaking about the Authority (enforced by the Witch). Campaign-level only.  
**Counterplay:** Narrative / GM-level interference only. Cannot be blocked by normal means.

## Sloth — Unseen Hand / Invisible Providence
**Users:** Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti (classic); Natsuki Subaru (weaker form).  
**Governing Stat:** Willpower (contested) or automatic for pure force application against non-Authority defenses.  
**Effect:** Invisible force limbs that can crush, restrain, or strike from any angle. Subaru’s version is weaker, fewer hands, and injures him when overused.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Sharing Factor portions (fingers) reduces the number of hands until reclaimed. Overuse risks possession or mental breakdown.  
**Counterplay:** Authority perception, spatial isolation, or incompatibility with certain possession effects.

## Greed — Lion’s Heart / Little King (Regulus Corneas)
**User:** Regulus Corneas.  
**Resolution:** Automatic while the external-heart condition holds.  
**Effect:** Complete invulnerability / “stop time on himself” (Lion’s Heart). Little King allows him to share the invulnerability with wives or objects under specific conditions.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Must keep his heart outside his body (usually in a wife). Isolation or destruction of the external heart ends the invulnerability.  
**Counterplay:** Sever the network of hearts or isolate Regulus from his anchors.

## Greed — Cor Leonis (Subaru)
**User:** Natsuki Subaru.  
**Resolution:** Automatic link after mutual recognition; Intelligence governs coordination and burden sharing.  
**Effect:** Locate linked allies and redistribute physical/mental burdens (pain, fatigue, etc.).  
**Cost / Limitations:** Requires willing or recognized connection. Overloading can harm Subaru.  
**Counterplay:** Break recognition or overwhelm the link capacity.

## Gluttony — Names and Memories
**Users:** Lye Batenkaitos, Roy Alphard, Louis / Rui Arneb (and later manifestations such as Spica).  
**Resolution:** Automatic only after specific identity + contact conditions are met.  
**Effect:** Consume a target’s name, memories, or both. Solar/Lunar Eclipse and later variants (Eclipsing Binary, Astral Eclipse) allow reproduction of stolen capabilities.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Requires correct name/identity knowledge and physical or conceptual contact. Identity contradictions can disrupt the Authority.  
**Counterplay:** Deny the conditions, exploit identity paradoxes, or use anti-Authority effects.

## Wrath — Sirius Romanée-Conti
**Governing Stat:** Charisma vs Willpower (propagation).  
**Effect:** Emotion/pain sharing and chain effects. Can force shared suffering or emotional states across linked targets.  
**Cost / Limitations:** Requires line of effect or established link. Overuse risks feedback.  
**Counterplay:** Willpower contests, isolation, or breaking emotional links.

## Lust — Capella Emerada Lugunica
**Governing Stat:** Charisma or Authority contest for unwilling targets.  
**Effect:** Body transformation, forced mutation, and “love” compulsion. Can reshape living beings (including herself).  
**Cost / Limitations:** Unwilling targets get a contest. Extreme transformations may have permanence or side effects.  
**Counterplay:** Contest the transformation, remove the Factor, or exploit her personality.

## Pride — Typhon
**Effect:** Judgment and “execution” based on the target’s own sense of guilt/sin. Can reduce beings to a child-like or powerless state if they are judged.  
**Resolution:** Largely automatic once judgment criteria are met; contested Willpower may apply for resistance.  
**Counterplay:** Lack of guilt, strong self-justification, or anti-Authority measures.

## Vainglory — Pandora
**Effect:** Reality rewriting / “as if it never happened” on a conceptual level. Extremely high-tier conceptual Authority.  
**Resolution:** Automatic under her conditions; exact limits remain **UNCERTAIN**.  
**Counterplay:** Extremely limited; treat as campaign-level threat.

## Melancholy — Hector / later users (Clind, Petra in certain loops)
**Effect:** Pressure, weight, and conceptual compression. Can crush with “gravity” of melancholy.  
**Resolution:** Contested or automatic depending on manifestation.  
**Counterplay:** Spatial freedom, high Willpower/Fortitude, or removing the Factor.

## Envy — Satella (Witch of Envy)
**Effect:** Infinite mana, immortality, shadow possession, and the eternal-draw condition tied to Subaru.  
**Resolution:** Largely automatic / conceptual.  
**Counterplay:** Narrative only at Tier 0.

## Other / Incomplete
- **Sekhmet (Sloth Witch):** Extreme Authority output that can overwhelm even other high-tier entities when fully unleashed.  
- New manifestations (e.g. Eclipsing Binary, Astral Eclipse) can appear after major perspective shifts.  
- Any Authority can evolve with the user’s mentality.
